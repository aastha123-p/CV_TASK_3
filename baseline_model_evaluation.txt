3. WRITTEN HYPOTHESIS ON THE WEAKEST CLASS (Leak)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Identified Weakest Class: Leak (mAP50 = 0.0826, mAP50-95 = 0.0231)

A. Diffuse Visual Boundaries & Non-Rigid Geometry
   Unlike structural voids (Hole) with high-contrast, sharp dark edges or 
   discolored oxidized patches (rust), Leak instances (liquid trails, surface 
   moisture, active spray, wet sheen) lack fixed structural shapes and rigid 
   edges. The boundary between a wet liquid streak and the surrounding metallic 
   pipe surface is diffuse and subtle, making localized spatial feature extraction 
   difficult for anchor-free convolutional backbones.

B. Severe Recall Deficit (9.1% Recall)
   While precision for Leak remains moderate (0.6480), the recall drops sharply 
   to 0.0909 (9.1%). This indicates that the baseline model misses over 90% of 
   true leak instances. The network defaults to extreme conservatism, failing 
   to activate standard confidence thresholds when encountering subtle sheen or 
   low-contrast fluid stains.

C. Co-Occurrence & Confusion with rust / Metallic Reflections
   Fluid leaks frequently co-occur directly over rust patches ("wet rust") or 
   emanate from punctures. Without explicit feature enhancement or contextual 
   loss weighting, standard YOLO feature pyramids confuse discolored rust edges 
   and background pipe reflections with fluid boundaries, attributing 
   predictions to rust rather than Leak.

D. High Intra-Class Variance & Limited Samples
   The Leak class encompasses multiple visually distinct subclasses—ranging 
   from reflective liquid droplets and flow streaks to wide residue rings and 
   active fine sprays. With only 22 validation instances and limited training 
   samples, the small baseline YOLOv8n network (3.0M parameters) lacks 
   sufficient capacity to generalize across all visual representations of 
   liquid emission.


4. RECOMMENDED IMPROVEMENT STRATEGIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Targeted Augmentations: Apply HSV saturation adjustments, contrast jitter, 
   and color transformations to decouple liquid sheen from pipe background colors.
2. Loss Function Adjustment: Increase classification loss weight (cls) and 
   incorporate Focal Loss / class weights to penalize Leak misclassifications.
3. Over-sampling & Data Balancing: Increase sampling frequency for images 
   containing Leak labels during training iterations.
