1. EXPERIMENTAL SETUP & IMPROVEMENTS APPLIED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Training Script: train_improved.py
Model Architecture: YOLOv8n (yolov8n.pt)
Output Path: runs/yolov8n_improved
Evaluation Dataset: dataset_450_yolo_improved/valid (67 images, 88 instances)

Applied Improvement Strategies:
1. Targeted Augmentations:
   - HSV-Saturation gain (hsv_s=0.8) and HSV-Value gain (hsv_v=0.6) to decouple 
     liquid sheen and moisture discoloration from background pipe metal.
   - Mixup (0.15) and Copy-Paste (0.3) augmentations to assist in learning diffuse 
     non-rigid boundaries of liquid trails.
   - Full spatial jittering: degrees=15.0, translate=0.1, scale=0.5, shear=2.0.

2. Loss Function Adjustment:
   - Classification loss weight boosted to cls=1.5 (3x over baseline default 0.5) 
     to directly penalize misclassifications and address the severe Leak recall drop.

3. Oversampling & Data Balancing:
   - Dataset oversampled: Images containing Leak labels were duplicated in the 
     training manifest, expanding training images from 316 to 429 images.
